Overview

What is GymRM?

A purpose-built multi-tenant SaaS for martial arts and combat sports gyms.

🏋️ Member Management
Full athlete database with photo, contact, emergency info, rank history, and guardian links.
📅 Class Scheduling
Weekly calendar with copy, multi-coach support, and capacity limits.
🖥️ Self-Service Kiosk
Athletes check in via QR code or NS number — no staff needed.
🛒 Store POS
Sell merchandise and drinks with a dedicated POS terminal and inventory tracking.
📊 Reports
Daily and monthly revenue by service type and payment method.
📣 Communications
SMS broadcasts and email to all members from inside the app.

Kiosk Role

Self-Service Kiosk Check-In

A dedicated check-in terminal at the gym entrance — athletes check in without staff assistance.

  • Runs on any browser (tablet, monitor, laptop)
  • QR code scan from Athlete ID card
  • Manual entry via NS number numpad
  • 30-minute cooldown prevents duplicate scans
  • Session does not expire — always-on
  • Password managed in Admin → Special Logins
Check-In Flow
Scan QR or enter NS number → Validate membership → Name confirmed on screen → Check-in logged → Screen resets.
