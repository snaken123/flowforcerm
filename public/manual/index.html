What is FlowForceRM GymRM?

GymRM is the all-in-one management system for FlowForceRM. It handles athlete records, class scheduling, check-ins, memberships, store sales, communications, and reporting — all from one place, on any device.

Who uses GymRM?

🛡️
Admin
Full access. Manages members, schedule, reports, store, employees, and settings.
🥋
Coach
Sees today's classes and class rosters. Read-only access to the schedule.
👤
Staff / Front Desk
Checks in athletes, looks up members, assigns memberships, manages schedule.
🖥️
Kiosk
Self-service check-in screen placed at the gym entrance. Members scan their QR or enter their athlete number.
🛒
Store
POS terminal access. Processes product sales, views inventory and sales reports.
🏃
Athlete (Member)
Views personal profile, class schedule, bookings, memberships, and athlete ID card.

How to open the app

  1. 1
    Open any web browser — Chrome, Safari, or Edge.
  2. 2
    Go to flowforcerm.com
  3. 3
    The Sign In page will appear. You can bookmark this page for quick access.
💡
Works on phones and tablets

GymRM works on any screen size. The layout adjusts automatically for mobile use.

How to log in

The FlowForceRM GymRM login screen
  1. 1
    Enter your Email address in the first field.
  2. 2
    Enter your Password in the second field.
  3. 3
    Click the blue Sign In button.
  4. 4
    You will be taken directly to your personal dashboard.
⚠️
Forgot your password?

Click the Forgot password? link on the login page. Enter your email and a reset link will be sent to your inbox.

In this section

1. Setting Up the Kiosk

The kiosk is a dedicated screen — typically a tablet or monitor at the gym entrance — that displays the check-in interface 24/7.

Kiosk Check-In screen — scanner ready, numpad for manual entry
  1. 1
    Open a browser on the kiosk device and go to flowforcerm.com
  2. 2
    Log in with the Kiosk account credentials (set in Admin → Settings → Special Logins).
  3. 3
    The kiosk check-in screen will appear automatically.
  4. 4
    Keep the browser in full-screen mode (press F11) and leave the device plugged in.
💡
The kiosk stays logged in

The kiosk session does not expire. Once set up, it will remain on the check-in screen until the browser is closed or the password is changed.

2. How Athletes Check In (Manual)

  1. 1
    The screen shows "Scanner Ready" with a numpad and an NS- field.
  2. 2
    The athlete enters their NS number using the numpad (e.g. 1234 for NS-1234).
  3. 3
    Press GO.
  4. 4
    If found, the athlete's name and membership are confirmed on screen.
  5. 5
    The check-in is logged and the screen resets after a few seconds.

3. QR Code Check-In

  1. 1
    The athlete opens their Athlete ID card in the GymRM Athlete portal on their phone.
  2. 2
    They hold their phone screen up to the QR scanner connected to the kiosk.
  3. 3
    The kiosk reads the code and checks them in instantly.
💡
No QR scanner?

The manual numpad always works as a fallback. Athletes can look up their NS number on their Athlete ID card in the app.

4. Kiosk Cooldown Rules

RuleDetail
Cooldown period30 minutes between kiosk check-ins for the same athlete
Why this existsPrevents accidental double scans
OverrideStaff can manually check in an athlete from the Staff Check-In screen if needed within the cooldown window

Store Guide

The Store POS is used to sell drinks, merchandise, and other gym products. Access is available to Admin, Staff, and Store role users.

In this section

  1. 1. Processing a sale (POS)
  2. 2. Managing inventory
  3. 3. Sales reports
  4. 4. Store terminal login

1. Processing a Sale (POS)

Store POS — New Sale tab with product cards and cart
  1. 1
    Click Store in the sidebar. The New Sale tab opens by default.
  2. 2
    Click a product card to add it to the cart on the right.
  3. 3
    Use + and − to change quantity, or the trash icon to remove an item.
  4. 4
    Select a payment method — Cash, GCash, Maya, Bank Transfer.
  5. 5
    Click Complete Sale. The cart clears and the sale is recorded.
💡
Special price

If a product is sold at a discount, enter the adjusted price before completing the sale. A justification reason is required for any price change.

2. Managing Inventory

Store — Inventory tab
  1. 1
    Inside the Store page, click the Inventory tab.
  2. 2
    Click + Add Product to create a new item.
  3. 3
    Set the product name, price, stock level, and image.
  4. 4
    Click the pencil icon on any product to edit it.
⚠️
Inventory management — Admin and Staff only

The Store role account can process sales but cannot add or edit products. Contact an Admin or Staff member to update inventory.

3. Sales Reports

Store — Sales Report tab (transaction history)

The Sales Report tab shows a full history of every transaction processed through the Store POS, including the date, items sold, quantity, total, and payment method.

1. Logging In as an Athlete

  1. 1
    Go to flowforcerm.com in your browser.
  2. 2
    Enter the email and password you registered with.
  3. 3
    Click Sign In.
  4. 4
    You are taken to your personal Member Dashboard.
💡
First time?

If you registered through a Free Trial, your account was created automatically. Use the email you registered with and the password from your confirmation email. If you forgot it, use Forgot password? on the login page.

2. Your Profile

Your profile shows your personal information, emergency contact, and photo. You can update most of these yourself.

3. Class Schedule & Bookings

View the full weekly class schedule and book yourself into classes that match your membership.

  1. 1
    Click Schedule in your sidebar.
  2. 2
    Browse the weekly calendar — classes show the coach name, time, and available spots.
  3. 3
    Click a class you want to join.
  4. 4
    Click Book to reserve your spot.
  5. 5
    Your booking appears in your personal schedule.
⚠️
Session-based memberships

If your membership has a limited number of sessions (e.g. 10-session Boxing package), one session is deducted each time you check in to a class. The system will warn you if you have 0 sessions remaining.

4. Your Athlete ID Card

Your Athlete ID card is a digital card with your photo, name, NS number, and a QR code for kiosk check-in.

  1. 1
    Tap Athlete ID in your sidebar.
  2. 2
    Your ID card is displayed with your QR code.
  3. 3
    At the kiosk, hold the QR code up to the scanner to check in instantly.
💡
Screenshot your ID card

Take a screenshot of your Athlete ID card and save it to your phone's home screen for fast kiosk check-in — no need to open the app every time.

6. Self Check-In (Kiosk)

  1. 1
    Walk up to the kiosk at the gym entrance.
  2. 2
    Either scan your QR code (from your Athlete ID card) or enter your NS number using the numpad.
  3. 3
    The screen will confirm your name and check-in. Done!

7. Free Trial Registration

New members can register for a free trial class online without visiting the gym first.

  1. 1
    Visit the Free Trial Registration link provided by FlowForceRM (available on the gym's social media or website).
  2. 2
    Fill in your name, email, phone number, and select a class type.
  3. 3
    Submit the form — your free trial account is created and a confirmation email is sent.
  4. 4
    Show up at the gym for your trial class. Staff will check you in.
✅
Free trial converts to full membership

If you decide to join after your trial, staff will assign a paid membership directly to your existing athlete account — no re-registration needed.
